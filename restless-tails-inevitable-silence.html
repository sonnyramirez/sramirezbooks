About the Book

Decisions end choices.

Call it coincidence or fate, whatever brought Archie and Mister Eric together was inevitable, and somewhere along the way they became brothers. Archie had left the streets behind for a new life in Clawton, but when his old organization comes looking for him, he has to choose between the life he built and the one he left. With Mister Eric and Floyd’s crew behind him, he goes after the operation, and this time he has more to lose than ever before.

Inevitable Silence is the third and final book in the Restless Tails trilogy.
