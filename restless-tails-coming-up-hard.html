About the Book

Raised by the state and taken over by the streets.

Archie was never given anything. When a feline stole his heart, he was ready to leave that life behind. Until that heart was shattered. Archie reacted the way he was raised, ruthlessly, and there was no going back. He took what he needed, and in the end, it wasn’t what he needed after all.

Coming Up Hard is the first book in the Restless Tails trilogy.
