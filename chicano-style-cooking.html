About the Book

From the streets to the stove.

I’ve always loved food. Growing up, it was my comfort, and I needed a lot of comfort. Then I went to the joint, and food became something else. Inside, you learn to make something out of nothing with a pack of ramen, a can of mackerel and whatever you can trade for.

This book is all of it. You’ll find my mom’s homemade tortillas, Aunt Lonnie’s refried beans, Dad’s goulash burritos and my wife’s turkey spaghetti, sitting right next to the spreads I made in a cell with a stinger and a plastic bag. Every recipe comes with the story behind the plate, including the ones I’m not proud of.

These are real recipes from kitchens that never had measuring cups, just memories and taste. If you’re reading this, you’re one of my people now.

Every recipe here has a story, and every story has a little bit of Chicano soul and street flavor.

What Is Inside

Breakfast Our Way: Homemade Tortillas, Papas and Weenies with Chorizo and Eggs, Homemade Pinto Beans, and Aunt Lonnie’s Refried Beans.

Lunch for the Wicked: Prison Lunch Spread, Chili & Salami Spread, and The Fish Spread.

Back to Reality Lunch Time: The Breakfast-Turned-Lunch Burrito and Dad’s Goulash Lunch Burrito.

Dinner Time, My Favorite Recipes: Homemade Puree Salsa, Homemade Chunky Salsa, Mom’s Homemade Tacos, Mom’s Meatloaf, Enchiladas, Fettuccine Alfredo, Tuna Casserole, Baked Fish, and My Wife’s Turkey Spaghetti.

Dessert Time: Cinnamon Roll Cake, Pumpkin Pie, and Apple Cake.
