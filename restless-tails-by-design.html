About the Book

If ordered, he would comply. If chosen, he would decide.

Mister Eric Randall is a disciplined cat, learning to control chaos with design. He learns to be patient and to see what others miss. Then he meets one cat, and his life takes a different route.

By Design is the second book in the Restless Tails trilogy.
