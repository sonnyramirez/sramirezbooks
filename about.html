About the Author

S. Ramirez

Author of the Restless Tails trilogy, the Two Doors In series, and 12 Steps to a Happy Marriage.

Author Bio

S. Ramirez writes character-driven fiction about survival, loyalty, consequence, and the choices that follow a character long after the moment has passed.

His Restless Tails trilogy blends anthropomorphic crime drama and animal noir with military tension and street survival. The stories move through gangs, prison, and old debts, and through the cost of trying to leave a violent life behind.

His Two Doors In series begins with Santino Ramos, a gritty coming-of-age prison novel and the first of three books.

He writes for readers who like characters that carry their past without asking for pity.

He also wrote 12 Steps to a Happy Marriage, a marriage humor book drawn from things that really happened between him and his wife. After twenty-three years together, he has learned one thing for sure: he likes being married more than he likes being right.
