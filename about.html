About the Author

S. Ramirez

Author of character-driven fiction, crime drama, animal noir, and stories built around survival, loyalty, family, and consequence.

Author Bio

S. Ramirez writes character-driven fiction about survival, loyalty, consequence, and the choices that follow a character long after the moment has passed.

His Restless Tails trilogy blends anthropomorphic crime drama, animal noir, military tension, street survival, and hard-earned emotional truth. The stories move through gangs, prison, covert systems, old debts, family wounds, and the cost of trying to leave a violent life behind.

S. Ramirez writes for readers who like grit, heart, consequence, and characters who carry their past without asking for pity.
